PROBLEM 1: Calculated Columns & Filtering
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Prompt:
Retrieve the title, cost, retail, and calculated profit (retail minus cost) for 
all books in the 'Computer' category. Sort the results by profit in descending order.

Query:
SELECT title, cost, retail, (retail - cost) AS profit
FROM book
WHERE category = 'Computer'
ORDER BY profit DESC;

 HANDS ON DATABASE          | 18.00 |  30.95 |  12.95

PROBLEM 7: Self-Joins & String Concatenation
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Prompt:
Find each customer who was referred by another customer. Display the customer's 
ID, full name (as customer_name), and the full name of the referring customer 
(as referred_by_name).

Query:
SELECT 
    c.customerno,
    c.firstname || ' ' || c.lastname AS customer_name,
    r.firstname || ' ' || r.lastname AS referred_by_name
FROM customer r
JOIN customer c ON r.customerno = c.referredby;

Output:
 customerno |  customer_name  | referred_by_name 
------------+-----------------+------------------
       1002 | TAMMY GILES     | BONNIE SMITH
       1003 | JAKE BENTLEY    | BONNIE SMITH
       1005 | MEG MASON       | JAKE BENTLEY
       1008 | STEVE MONROE    | TAMMY GILES
       1010 | HARRY POTTER    | MEG MASON
(5 rows)

PROBLEM 8: Multi-Table Specific Order Filtering
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Prompt:
Display all line items for Order 1007. Include order number, item number, book 
title, quantity ordered, and retail price. Sort by item number.

Query:
SELECT o.orderno, oi.itemno, b.title, oi.quantity, b.retail
FROM orders o
JOIN orderitem oi ON o.orderno = oi.orderno
JOIN book b ON oi.isbn = b.isbn
WHERE o.orderno = 1007
ORDER BY oi.itemno;

Output:

    1007 |      2 | HOLY GRAIL         |        1 |  25.00
    1007 |      3 | REVENGE OF MICKEY  |        1 |  22.00

PROBLEM 9: Grouping & HAVING Clause Filtering
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Prompt:
Find all customers who have placed at least 2 orders. Display customer ID, 
first name, last name, and total order count. Sort by order count descending, 
then customer ID ascending.

Query:
SELECT c.customerno, c.firstname, c.lastname, COUNT(o.orderno) AS order_count
FROM customer c
JOIN orders o ON c.customerno = o.customerno
GROUP BY c.customerno, c.firstname, c.lastname
HAVING COUNT(o.orderno) >= 2
ORDER BY COUNT(o.orderno) DESC, c.customerno ASC;

Output:
 customerno | firstname | lastname | order_count 
------------+-----------+----------+-------------
       1001 | BONNIE    | SMITH    |           3
       1002 | TAMMY     | GILES    |           3
       1003 | JAKE      | BENTLEY  |           3
       1005 | MEG       | MASON    |           2
       1011 | STEVE     | MONROE   |           2
(5 rows)

PROBLEM 10: Complex Multi-Table Line Item Totals
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Prompt:
Generate a detailed receipt breakdown for Order 1012. Show full customer name, 
order date, book title, quantity, retail price, and calculated item_total. Sort 
descending by item total.

Query:
SELECT 
    c.firstname || ' ' || c.lastname AS customer_name,
    o.orderdate,
    b.title,
    oi.quantity,
    b.retail,
    (oi.quantity * b.retail) AS item_total
FROM orders o
JOIN customer c ON o.customerno = c.customerno
JOIN orderitem oi ON o.orderno = oi.orderno
JOIN book b ON oi.isbn = b.isbn
WHERE o.orderno = 1012
ORDER BY item_total DESC;

Output:
 customer_name | orderdate  |       title        | quantity | retail | item_total 
---------------+------------+--------------------+----------+--------+------------
 JAKE BENTLEY  | 2009-04-03 | DB: DATABASE DESIGN|        3 |  59.95 |     179.85
 JAKE BENTLEY  | 2009-04-03 | COOKING WITH BONNIE|        1 |  25.00 |      25.00
(2 rows)
